These are the 24 landmarks where no question in the level's subject rests on the sentence the landmark tells (ADR-0036 §2.4). Since ADR-0048 (slice L1a) they ask nothing when no task step is being played; with a task running they ask the step's pool.

**Most of this list is not debt (ADR-0056, 2026-09-17).** The product owner ruled that relatedness is a preference: *"if it matches to have a POI with a content related to that POI, that's great… else, it is still ok."* A landmark the guide says nothing about, teaching guide material from its level's subject anyway, is the **acceptable case** and nothing is owed on it. **12 of the 24 rows are that case** — the six marked *None found* (`warming-hut`, `grain-bins`, `combine-harvester`, `bulk-carrier`, `footbridge`, `autumn-maple`) and the six marked *Skipped*, each of which was skipped because the question would have graded one proposition in two subjects or put a second card on one memory. Those twelve **stop being tracked as work owed**. Do not close them by stretching a guide sentence onto a landmark it is not about: a strained claim is worse than an honest silence, and ADR-0048 already made the silence safe by having such a stop ask nothing rather than something unrelated.

**What remains owed is the opportunity half:** the rows where a granted question now rests on a sentence the landmark could genuinely tell, and the blurb still points at a different one. Re-pointing those blurbs is what makes the stop ask about what it just told. That is a **content-author** pass, then a separate **content-verifier** pass, and it carries a dated obligation in ADR-0056.

**Status, 2026-09-15** (branch `landmark-questions`, PR #39). The content-author pass has run. 12 rows were authored with a new id, and 6 are marked **Skipped** with the reason. The 6 rows with no proposition are unchanged. The verifier granted 10 of the 12 and rejected 2 (`bb214b3`). On branch `landmark-questions-fixes` the author marked `sym-43` volatile and re-filed `mc-41` under economy as `eco-51`. Both wait for the verifier again, and the `container-car` gap stays open for `modern-canada`. An authored question rests on the sentence in the proposition column, not on the blurb's own sentence. So it is not asked first at its landmark until that blurb is re-pointed to the same sentence (the first rule below), and re-pointing a verified blurb voids its grant. That re-pointing is still owed. The one exception is `spruce-stand`: the sternwheeler's quote already contains its sentence.

**ADR-0057 does not reopen this list (2026-09-17).** "A level asks only what it taught" binds what a question may *key* — its `source.quote` against the propositions the level tells — and not which landmark stands beside it. A level may teach a proposition at the train window and ask about it four stops later. So relatedness stays a preference, the twelve acceptable rows stay closed, a stop that asks nothing outside a task stays a legitimate end state, and the twelve opportunity rows keep the obligation ADR-0056 gave them. What ADR-0057 adds is a separate and larger debt — the teaching itself — recorded in slice L3 above.

## Rules
- A level must be addable by JSON and assets alone. If a level needs engine changes, the schema is wrong —
  fix the schema first (that is what slice 2 proves).
- Content slices touch only `content/` and can run beside level work.
- Nothing after slice 1 starts until slice 1 is live on Pages with `verify-art` and `verify-content` green.
- A gate is not trusted until it has been seen to fail on a real violation. Slice 0's audit found three that
  measured nothing and two skipped tests that asserted `0 === 0`; assume the next one is in here too.
