# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This project is built for newcomers to Canada. We make participation in it free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language, and plain language wherever possible.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences, especially about the cultures this game depicts.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly.
- Focusing on what is best for learners.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory jokes, personal or political attacks, publishing
others' private information, caricature or mockery of the peoples and cultures represented in this project,
and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainers listed in `SECURITY.md`.
Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban contributors, for
behaviour they consider inappropriate. Enforcement decisions aim to protect learners and contributors first.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
